Privacy Policy

Financial Decision Analyzer
Effective date: September 14, 2026

Financial Decision Analyzer ("FDA", "the App") is a financial decision analysis and calculation application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Financial Decision Analyzer does not collect personal information from users.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, address, financial account information, or other personal information.

The App does not collect or transmit financial information to us or to a server.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store certain information locally on your device to provide its functionality.

This may include:

  • App preferences, such as currency and theme settings
  • Saved financial scenarios, including information you enter into those scenarios
  • Scenario labels and dates associated with saved scenarios

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Financial Decision Analyzer or to our servers.

3. Data Sharing

Financial Decision Analyzer does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The App does not use advertising services, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or other third-party services that collect user data.

The App does not transmit your saved scenarios or financial calculations to external servers.

Sharing a Scenario

The App may provide a sharing function that uses your device's operating-system share sheet.

If you choose to share a scenario or calculation, the information you choose to share may be sent to the application or service you select through your device's sharing functionality. This sharing is initiated by you, and the receiving application or service is responsible for its own privacy practices.

4. Internet and Network Access

Financial Decision Analyzer is designed to operate offline.

The production version of the App does not require internet access to perform its calculations or store saved scenarios.

The App does not operate a server that receives or stores user financial information.

5. Analytics and Advertising

Financial Decision Analyzer does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Personalized advertising
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics services
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Tracking technologies

6. Financial Information

Financial Decision Analyzer allows you to enter financial information for the purpose of performing calculations and comparing financial scenarios.

This information is processed locally on your device. It is not collected by the App developer or transmitted to our servers.

Financial Decision Analyzer is a calculation and analysis tool. It does not provide personalized financial advice, investment management, banking services, lending services, or other financial services.

The results produced by the App are estimates based on the information and assumptions you enter. They do not guarantee future financial or investment outcomes.

7. Children's Privacy

Financial Decision Analyzer is not specifically directed toward children.

Because the App does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Because Financial Decision Analyzer does not collect or transmit user information to our servers, there is no developer-controlled database containing user financial information.

Information saved locally by the App is subject to the security of the user's device and operating system. Users should use appropriate device security measures, such as a screen lock and current operating-system updates.

9. Third-Party Services

Financial Decision Analyzer does not use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

The operating system may provide system functionality, such as the device's share sheet. Use of third-party applications or services through those system functions is governed by the privacy policies of those applications or services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or its privacy practices.

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be published at this location with an updated effective date.
